## Returns and Refunds
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Laptops have a shorter return window of 14 days from delivery.

Returned items must be in their original packaging with all accessories included. Gift cards and opened software licenses cannot be returned.

Opened laptops returned without a defect are subject to a 15% restocking fee.

Return shipping is free if the item is defective or we sent the wrong item. In all other cases the customer pays for return shipping, and a $7.99 label fee is deducted from the refund.

To start a return, go to My Orders, choose the order, click Request Return, and print the prepaid label. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. We do not offer direct exchanges. To swap an item, return it and place a new order.

## Warranty
All Acme Gadgets products come with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date. Batteries are covered for 6 months only.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage (except for products with a water resistance rating used within their rating), or repairs made by anyone other than Acme Gadgets.

Acme Care+ is an optional protection plan. It adds 2 extra years of coverage (3 years total) and covers accidental damage, with one accidental damage claim allowed per year. Acme Care+ costs 12% of the product price and must be purchased within 30 days of buying the product.

To make a warranty claim, email warranty@acmegadgets.example with your order number, a description of the problem and photos or a short video. We will repair, replace or refund the product within 10 business days of approving the claim.

## Troubleshooting

### Earbuds will not pair
Put both Pulse X1 earbuds in the charging case and close the lid. Hold the button on the case for 10 seconds until the LED flashes white. Remove Pulse X1 from the Bluetooth list on your phone, then pair again.

### Laptop will not turn on
Charge the Orbit Pro 14 with the included charger for at least 30 minutes. Then hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a hard reset and try turning it on again. If it still does not start, submit a warranty claim.

### Smartwatch battery drains quickly
Turn off the always-on display, lower the screen brightness and disable continuous heart-rate monitoring. Update the firmware in the Acme Wear app. Normal battery life is about 5 days. If the battery lasts less than 2 days after these steps, it may qualify for a battery warranty claim.

### Charger feels warm
It is normal for the Volt 65W charger to feel warm during fast charging. If it is too hot to touch or you smell burning, unplug it immediately and contact support.

### Webcam is not detected
Open the privacy shutter on the Beam 4K Webcam, try a different USB-C port or cable, and close any other app that may be using the camera.
